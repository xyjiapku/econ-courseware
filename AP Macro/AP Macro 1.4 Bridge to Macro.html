Summary

Key takeaways

① P ▲ → Qd ▼ · P ▲ → Qs ▲.

② Own price → movement along; non-price factors → shift.

③ P above P* → surplus → price falls; P below P* → shortage → price rises.

④ Double shift: one variable certain, the other indeterminate.

⑤ D slopes down from substitution; AD slopes down from wealth / interest-rate / foreign-purchase.

⑥ Marginal = the change (Δ) → MPC = ΔC/ΔY, MPS = ΔS/ΔY, sum = 1.
