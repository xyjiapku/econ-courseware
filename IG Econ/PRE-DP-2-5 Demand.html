HOW A LAW IS BORN · A PHYSICS EXAMPLE

Hooke's Law — from data to a law

1660s, Robert Hooke hangs weights on a spring: "Ut tensio, sic vis" — stretch ∝ force. How did he work it out?

He controls the spring (same one), changes the force, records the stretch — step by step.

F = kx

Force rises linearly with stretch. Same shape every time → that is a LAW.

HOW A LAW IS BORN · TWO LAWS, ONE LOGIC

Side by side — physics and economics

Two different sciences. One identical recipe. That's why the lines and the predictions look the same.

🔬 Hooke's Law

① Control: same spring

② Change: force F

③ Record: stretch x

④ Fit: F = kx · slope = k (stiffness)

Same shape, every time you stretch a spring.

📈 Law of Demand

① Control: ceteris paribus — other things equal

② Change: price P

③ Record: quantity demanded Qd

④ Fit: Qd falls as P rises · negative slope

Same shape, every time the canteen raises a price.

Notice ① — economists even borrow the word control from physics: ceteris paribus. Remember it — it decides whether the curve moves along or shifts.

DEMAND CURVE · 绘制

Drawing the demand curve

Step 1: plot the points. Step 2: join them. Step 3: slide the price and watch the dot ride the curve.

Price slider · 拖动价格

P = ¥25 → Qd = 6 cups

What the curve tells us:

Every point = one price and the quantity demanded at that price.

Price ↓ → slide down: EXTENSION 需求量扩张
Price ↑ → slide up: CONTRACTION 需求量收缩

The curve itself never moves — only our position on it. 曲线不动，动的只是位置（什么变了？只有价格）。

FACTORS ① INCOME · 收入变化

Income changes — normal vs inferior goods

The most important shift factor. But the direction depends on the TYPE of good · 方向取决于商品类型。

Normal goods · 正常物品

Income ↑ → demand rises (shift RIGHT) · 收入↑需求↑

e.g. milk tea, new phones, eating out · 奶茶、新手机、外出就餐

Inferior goods · 劣等商品

Income ↑ → demand falls (shift LEFT) · 收入↑需求反而↓

e.g. instant noodles, bus rides, second-hand clothes · 方便面、公交、二手衣

"Inferior" is not an insult — it just means students switch away from it when they get richer. 劣等 ≠ 差，只是有钱后就不买了。

记住这对方向 · memorise: Normal → income ↑ = shift right；Inferior → income ↑ = shift left. 方向随商品类型反转。

FACTORS ② RELATED GOODS · 相关产品价格

Substitutes & complements

替代品与互补品 — the price of a RELATED good shifts your curve too.

Substitutes · 替代品

Goods that replace each other: Coke & Pepsi, tea & coffee, bus & metro.

Substitute's price ↑ → our demand ↑ (shift RIGHT)

百事涨价 → 大家改喝可口可乐

Complements · 互补品

Goods used together: game console & games, printer & ink, phones & apps.

Complement's price ↑ → our demand ↓ (shift LEFT)

游戏机涨价 → 游戏的需求也减少

记住这对方向 · memorise: Substitute price ↑ = shift right；Complement price ↑ = shift left. 方向相反，考试爱考。

FACTORS ③ THE REST · 其余因素

Advertising, population, tastes — and more

零碎但常考 · each one shifts the WHOLE curve.

📢 Advertising · 广告

A successful campaign makes the brand popular → demand shifts right.

👥 Population · 人口

More people (or more students in school) → demand shifts right.

👗 Tastes & fashion · 品味时尚

Trendy → right; out of fashion → left.

…and more · 其它因素

Weather 天气（hot summer → ice cream）· Expectations 预期（expect prices to rise → buy today）· Special days 特殊日子（Valentine's → roses）— all shift demand right here.

All of these move the WHOLE curve — never a movement along it. 这些因素移动整条需求曲线。
