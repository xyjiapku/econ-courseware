FROM FIRMS TO GOVERNMENT · 引入

The same number, a new decision-maker

Firms use PED on prices. Governments use it on taxes — and PED decides who really pays.

Firms — recap 💼

Elastic demand → a price cut pays. Brand loyalty → inelastic → pricing power.

Government 🏛️

Revenue → tax inelastic goods. Discourage a good → tax it when demand is elastic.

The question of the day

Coursebook: the incidence of taxation refers to the distribution of an indirect tax shared between consumers and firms.

Legal incidence: the firm hands the money over. Economic incidence: who is really worse off — shared.

Milk-tea market: ¥30 / 5 cups. Tax ¥20 a unit — big enough to see every block.

STEP 1 · 供给

Start with the market — then add the tax

Market first, tax second. Watch the curve move — then the quantity.

⓪ The market before the tax

D and S cross at ¥30 / 5 cups — the base every later figure is measured against.

① A cost → supply shifts

¥20 more to supply each unit → S up ¥20 → S + tax. (Sideways: 4 cups to the left.)

Demand unmoved — tastes unchanged. Only the cost of supplying changed.

② The market shrinks

New equilibrium on S + tax: 5 → 3 cups — two cups priced out.

STEP 2 · 价格

One price becomes two

Buyers pay more, sellers keep less. The gap between them is the tax.

① Buyers pay: ¥40

Was ¥30. Now ¥40 → ¥10 more.

② Sellers keep: ¥20

Of the ¥40: ¥20 to the government, ¥20 kept → ¥10 less.

The ¥20 gap = the tax. Two prices, one market — always say which.

STEP 3 · 收入

The government's revenue is a rectangle

Tax per unit × units still sold. Height = the tax, width = what is left of the market.

① Read the rectangle

¥20 × 3 cups = ¥60 — all the government collects.

Taller tax → narrower market. Past about ¥25 revenue starts to fall.

Tax per unit: ¥20

¥0¥30

Reveal both steps — then drag the tax.

STEP 4 · 分摊

Split the rectangle — who really pays

Cut the block at the ORIGINAL price. Above → what buyers pay extra. Below → what sellers lose.

① Consumers' share

¥10 extra × 3 cups = ¥30 → the top half.

② Producers' share

¥10 less × 3 cups = ¥30 → the bottom half.

Consumers ¥30 · 50%
Producers ¥30 · 50%

D and S equally steep → 50 : 50. Change D's steepness and the split moves.

THE RULE · 规律

The side that cannot escape pays more

What you just watched, in two lines. Supply held still throughout.

Demand INELASTIC (steep D)

Buyers cannot walk away → firms pass the tax on → consumers bear most.

Demand ELASTIC (shallow D)

Buyers walk away → firms absorb it → producers bear most.

Coursebook: "In the case of products with inelastic demand, consumers bear most of the tax… if products have elastic demand, it is the firms who bear most of the tax."

One line: the tax lands on whoever has fewer alternatives — the steeper curve. Supply elasticity works the same way (PES — next lesson).
