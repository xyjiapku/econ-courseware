Banking without interest

EXTENSION
Islamic finance forbids riba — interest. Four ways round it:
Murabaha
cost-plus

The bank buys the goods and resells at a stated mark-up.

Mudarabah
profit-sharing

Bank provides capital, firm does the work.

Musharakah
partnership

Both put in capital and share the risk.

Ijara
leasing

The bank owns the asset and rents it out.

Riyadh · a Sharia-compliant market

Same three functions, different form — but the saver gets a share of profit, not a fixed rate.

Why a bank run is frightening

MECHANISM
A bank keeps only a fraction of deposits as cash — that is how lending works, not fraud.

China, 2019. Baoshang Bank ran out of cash; the PBOC took it over and guaranteed the deposits.

Every ¥100 deposited:
12
88 lent out

Cash stays liquid; loans come back in years. That is the business model.

If everyone asks on the same day, the bank is illiquid, not insolvent — so the central bank lends.

Confidence, not cash, is what keeps a bank standing
