Step 2

On · Inside · Outside

先看坐标思考 → 显示点 → 再解释
Each hour yields 10 fish or 10 coconuts — at most 8 hours.   A (40, 40) · B (20, 20) · C (60, 40) — how many hours did he work at each point, and where does each point sit?

A (40, 40) · On the curve

4 h + 4 h = 8 hours ✓ all time used — efficient (full employment)

B (20, 20) · Inside

2 h + 2 h = 4 hours — time not fully used — inefficient (waste)

C (60, 40) · Outside

6 h + 4 h = 10 hours — unattainable (impossible) with 8 hours

Key idea: on = full use = efficient = full employment · inside = waste = inefficient · outside = impossible = unattainable
Step ① — read the three coordinates above and think first.
Step 3

Slope = Opportunity Cost

沿 PPC 移动：斜率就是机会成本

Move along the line

From A (10, 70) to B (40, 40): + 30 fish → − 30 coconuts

Opportunity cost of 30 fish = 30 coconuts
= 1 coconut per fish (constant)
Slope = ΔCoconuts / ΔFish = 30 / 30 = 1
👉 The slope of the PPC is the opportunity cost

Tips

Drawing the PPC — Common Mistakes

画图常见错误 · AP 评分口径

✅ Do this

① Label both axes — Fish and Coconuts.

② Shape matches the question — straight = constant OC · bowed out = increasing OC · convex = decreasing OC.

③ Mark the numbers at the two endpoints — where the curve meets each axis, write the given maximum quantities.

❌ Avoid these

① Don't label every intermediate point — endpoints + correct shape are what earn the marks; extra point labels waste time.

② Curve stops in mid-air — the ends must touch the axes.

③ Wrong bend — bowed out = increasing OC, bowed in (convex) = decreasing OC. Check which one the question describes.

AP Micro exam tip: when the question gives numbers, mark the two axis intercepts with those numbers and get the shape right — you do NOT need to label every point on the curve.

Summary

Key Ideas

本课要点

What is a PPC?

Maximum combinations of two goods an economy can produce with all resources fully used — from Robinson's table to the graph.

Three key ideas

① Straight → constant OC · bowed out → increasing OC · convex → decreasing OC
② Slope = opportunity cost — the gradient is what you give up
③ On = full use = efficient = full employment
   Inside = waste = inefficient
   Outside = impossible = unattainable (with current resources & technology)

Drawing checklist

Label axes ✓ · Ends touch axes ✓ · Shape matches the story ✓ · Endpoints numbered ✓

Remember: to produce more of one good, you give up another — that is opportunity cost, and the PPC shows it.
