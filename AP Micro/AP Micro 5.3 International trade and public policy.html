DEFINITIONS · 定义

Four words you must keep apart

Two describe the state of the border; two name the tools that change it.

Free trade

Nothing at the border. The home price settles at the world price; the gap is filled by imports — or sold abroad as exports.

Protection

Any policy that holds the home price away from the world price — up, in the import case.

Tariff

A tax per unit on imports. The rectangle tariff × imports goes to the government.

Quota

A legal cap on the quantity allowed across. The same rectangle goes to whoever holds the licence, as rent.

What separates them: a tariff raises revenue for the state; a quota creates rent for a licence holder. The price and quantity effects can be identical.

IMPORTS · 进口

World price below $30 — five steps

One market, five steps: home price → world price → CS → PS → the net gain.

1 · Start closed. D meets S at 5 units, $30 — CS $62.5 + PS $62.5 = total $125.

2 · The world price. Pw = $20, below our $30 → buyers want 7, sellers offer 3, imports 4.

3 · Consumer surplus. 62.5 → 122.5 — the outlined block is +$60.

4 · Producer surplus. 62.5 → 22.5 — the hatched block is −$40.

5 · The net gain. +60 − 40 = +$20, the trade triangle. Total 125 → 145.

EXPORTS · 出口

World price above $30 — the same five steps

Now Pw sits above $30. Same market, same steps — the winners simply swap places.

1 · Start closed. D meets S at 5 units, $30 — CS $62.5 + PS $62.5 = total $125.

2 · The world price. Pw = $40, above our $30 → sellers offer 7, buyers want 3, exports 4.

3 · Consumer surplus. 62.5 → 22.5 — the hatched block is −$40.

4 · Producer surplus. 62.5 → 122.5 — the outlined block is +$60.

5 · The net gain. −40 + 60 = +$20, the same triangle. Total 125 → 145.

ECONOMIC RENT · 经济租

Economic rent — paid above the alternative

The quota handed its licence holders $10 for producing nothing. Where does a payment like that come from — and what is it called?

1 · The quota rent. Imports held at 2 while the home price sat $5 above the world price: 2 × $5 = $10 to the licence holder, for nothing.

2 · Split any payment in two. The least an owner would accept = transfer earnings. Anything on top = economic rent.

3 · So producer surplus is rent — the area above S. The area below S is transfer earnings. Under the $5 tariff that is $40.

4 · Fixed supply ⇒ all of it is rent. Stadium, superstar, rare-earth licence: transfer earnings ≈ 0, so the whole payment is rent. (Not the rent a landlord collects — same word, different thing.)

WINNERS · 输赢

Who gains, who loses

The direction depends on whether the country imports or exports — switch and watch the signs flip.

Consumers win. The home price falls to the world price, so households pay less: CS 62.5 → 122.5.

Producers lose — and so do their towns. The US Rust Belt: steel, autos, and the communities built around them. PS 62.5 → 22.5.

Why it becomes politics. Millions gain a little; a few hundred thousand lose a lot. Concentrated losses organise; diffuse gains do not.

Producers win. The home price rises to the world price: PS 62.5 → 122.5.

Consumers lose. They now pay the world price at home: CS 62.5 → 22.5. Exporting is not automatically good news for everyone.

Case · India. The largest rice exporter, over 40% of world trade, banned non-basmati exports on 20 July 2023 — exporting had pushed retail prices up 11.5%.

Both directions raise the total — but the winners swap places. Protection throws part away: free 145 > protected 140 > closed 125.

POLICY · 政策

The case for and against protection

Know both sides — and know what test settles it.

Arguments for protection

Infant industry · national security (food, fuel, chips) · jobs · anti-dumping.

What it costs

Higher prices at home, an industry that never grows up, a deadweight loss — and usually retaliation.

The economist's test

Does it fix a market failure, or merely move surplus? Only the first raises the total.

Trade is not zero-sum. Two countries can both gain — but not every group inside them does. Protect jobs is about who; the total falls is about how much.

CASE 1 · 关税

The Trump tariffs (2018–)

The textbook import tariff — and the textbook result.

The policy. 2018: 25% on steel, 10% on aluminium — then tariffs on hundreds of billions of dollars of Chinese goods. China hit back with 25% on US soybeans.

Read it with the model. The home price rises above the world price. Domestic producers gain, the treasury collects tariff × imports — households and steel-using firms pay more.

The mirror. Chinese soybean farmers lost as the US price fell. A tariff does not only tax foreigners.

Where it lands: Pd above Pw, imports shrunk, the revenue rectangle to the treasury — and two triangles of deadweight loss.

CASE 2 · 配额

The beef import quota (2026–2028)

The same protection — delivered by a cap instead of a tax.

The safeguard. Opened Dec 2024; on 31 Dec 2025 MOFCOM found surging imports had injured home producers and set country quotas — 55% above them — for 2026–2028.

Why it bites. Beef imports +73% (vs 2019); the 2026 cap of 2.688 Mt sits just under actual imports, so it binds.

Read it. The cap lifts the home price just like a tariff — but the rectangle becomes rent for licence holders, not revenue for the state.

Tariff or quota? Same price, same imports, same deadweight loss. What changes is who gets the rectangle.

HISTORY · 历史

Protection: then and now

Same tool, different motive — and the motive changed.

Then — the money motive

Mercantilism counted bullion, and customs duties were the easiest tax to collect. Tariffs were fiscal long before they were protective.

Now — the jobs motive

Tariff revenue is a rounding error now. The argument is industry and jobs — and lately security.

Four beats

1500s–1700s

Mercantilism: pile up bullion; customs is the revenue.

1846

Corn Laws repealed — Britain goes free trade.

1930

Smoot–Hawley: retaliation, then trade collapses.

Today

Revenue is trivial; the case is jobs and security.

REFLECTION · 反思

What comparative advantage assumes away

Ricardo proves that trade raises the total. These four are the price of the proof.

Costless adjustment

The proof moves labour and capital between industries free of charge. Real people cannot retrain at zero cost.

Given technology

Productivity is taken as fixed. But specialising today can lock a country into a low-productivity path — the infant-industry case.

Safe dependence

A foreign supplier is treated as a price, never as a risk. Food, fuel and chips can be cut off — no price carries that.

Diffuse gains

The theorem says the country gains, not that every group does. Millions gain a little; a few hundred thousand lose a lot.

Free trade raises the total — that is the whole claim. The political fight is over who pays for the adjustment.

GRAPH · 作图

What the trade graph must show

Four marks decide whether the graph carries the argument. Reveal them one at a time — the graph draws itself alongside.

1 · Two price lines. Pw and Pd = Pw + tariff — the gap between them is the tariff per unit.

2 · Both quantities. Qs and Qd at the domestic price — the horizontal gap between them is the trade volume.

3 · Shade the areas. Tariff × imports = the revenue rectangle; the two triangles between the curves are the DWL.

4 · Who pays it. A small country takes Pw as given, so the whole tariff lands on home buyers.

Before you move on: every line labelled · every asked-for area shaded · the Qd–Qs gap marked.
