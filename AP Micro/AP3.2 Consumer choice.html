Step 2

The Budget Line

预算线

Where it hits the axes

All income on soda: S = 300 ÷ 3 = 100.
All income on hamburgers: H = 300 ÷ 5 = 60.

Its slope

S = 100 − (5/3)H, so the slope is −PH/PS = −5/3.
The relative price: one hamburger costs 1⅔ sodas.

Three zones

On the line: money exactly spent. Below: affordable but money left over. Above: not affordable.

Step 3

What Moves the Budget Line

平移与转动

Income changes → parallel shift

I = $300 → $390: both intercepts rise by 30%, the slope is unchanged. Income down → the line shifts inward.

A price change → rotation

PH $5 → $3 (soda unchanged): the soda intercept stays at 100, the hamburger intercept jumps to 100, and the slope changes.

Step 5

The Marginal Rate of Substitution

边际替代率
Zoom in on one point of an indifference curve. Take one hamburger away — how many sodas must be added back to stay equally happy? That number is the MRS.

① The bundle

12 hamburgers and 42.7 sodas — utility U = 10. This is the point we zoom in on.

② One hamburger goes

With 11 hamburgers and the same soda, he is below his curve — worse off.

③ Add sodas back

He must add 1.9 sodas to return to U = 10 → MRS ≈ 1.9 at that point.

④ And it falls: one hamburger further right (12 → 13) costs only 1.8 sodas → MRS diminishes → the curve must be convex. And MRS = MUH ÷ MUS.
Step ① — start from one point on the curve.
Step 6

Four Properties of Indifference Curves

曲线性质
These four properties are what the exam expects you to draw and explain.

1 · Higher is better

A curve further from the origin means more of at least one good, so utility is higher.

2 · Downward sloping

To keep utility constant, more of one good means less of the other.

3 · Convex (bowed in)

Diminishing MU makes MRS fall as you move right — the curve flattens.

4 · They never cross

Crossing would mean two different utility levels at one bundle — impossible.

U = √H + √S → diminishing MU → convex

U = H + S → constant MU → straight lines

U = H² + S² → increasing MU → bowed the other way

Step ① — why must a higher curve mean more utility?

Step 8

Why the Two Rules Are the Same Rule

相切 ⇒ 两边斜率相等
At the best bundle the budget line and the indifference curve touch: same slope at that point. Write both slopes and the exam formula falls out.

① The slope of the indifference curve

−MUHMUS  =  −MRS

To keep utility unchanged, giving up sodas must be paid for with hamburgers — in the ratio of their marginal utilities.

② The slope of the budget line

−PHPS

The relative price: how many sodas one more hamburger costs in the market.

③ Tangency: set them equal

MUHMUS = PHPS
⇓
MUHPH = MUSPS
That is the rule AP tests: the last dollar spent on each good must give the same utility. Worked with whole units it becomes “buy until MU ÷ P is equal on both sides”.

Summary

What You Must Be Able to Do

总结 · MU/P 与解题流程

1 · The formula

MUXPX = MUYPY   

The last dollar spent on each good must buy the same utility. If MUXPX is larger, buy more X and less Y.

2 · The procedure

① turn TU into MU (ΔTU) → ② divide by the price → ③ buy the unit with the highest MU ÷ P → ④ stop when the two and the money is gone → ⑤ check i·PX + j·PY = income.
